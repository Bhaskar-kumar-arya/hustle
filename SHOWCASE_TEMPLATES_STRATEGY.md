# Dynamic Showcase & Demo Strategy for Bangalore Leads

## 1. The Core Strategy: The "Chameleon" Concept

Instead of manually building hundreds of separate websites for every scraped business lead, we use a **Dynamic Presentation Engine**. 

A single lightweight demo engine serves **4 Master Industry Archetypes**. When a prospect opens a preview link, the page dynamically injects their business data into the presentation in real time.

```
Incoming Lead Data (Name, Locality, Reviews, Phone, Niche)
                           ↓
             Dynamic Parameter Injection
                           ↓
       Tailored Industry Archetype Displayed (0s Build Time)
```

---

## 2. The 4 Master Industry Archetypes

Every local business in Bangalore fits into one of four primary transactional categories based on what their customers look for:

### Archetype 1: Clinical & Healthcare
* **Target Niches:** Dental Clinics, Dermatologists, Cosmetology, Ayurvedic & Panchakarma Centers, Physiotherapy, Pet Clinics / Vets.
* **Core Presentation Focus:**
  * Doctor credentials & clinical specializations.
  * Direct appointment scheduling & emergency WhatsApp hotline.
  * Patient confidence signals (Google review badges, hygiene standards).
  * Treatment explanations (e.g., Root Canals, Laser Therapy, Panchakarma packages).

### Archetype 2: Visual Portfolio & Spaces
* **Target Niches:** Interior Designers, Modular Kitchen Studios, Architects, Property Consultancies.
* **Core Presentation Focus:**
  * High-ticket project showcase & before/after room transformations.
  * 3D visualization / blueprint inquiry mechanism.
  * Design process walkthrough (Consultation ➔ 3D Model ➔ Handover).
  * Direct "Request a Custom Quote" funnel.

### Archetype 3: Beauty, Wellness & Personal Services
* **Target Niches:** Luxury Salons, Bridal Makeup Artists, Spas, CrossFit & Gyms, Yoga Studios.
* **Core Presentation Focus:**
  * Service rate cards & bridal / seasonal package breakdowns.
  * Visual gallery of transformations & treatments.
  * Instant WhatsApp slot / chair booking.
  * Membership pricing tiers or free trial session claims.

### Archetype 4: Professional, Commercial & Event Services
* **Target Niches:** Wedding Caterers, Event Planners, CA & Tax Consultants, Law Firms, Coaching Academies, Car Detailing Studios.
* **Core Presentation Focus:**
  * Digital menu / service catalog downloads.
  * Package cost estimators (e.g., per-plate caterer cost, ceramic coating tiers).
  * Testimonials & corporate trust credentials.
  * Direct consultation & booking hotline.

## 3. Dynamic Data Mapping (How Combinations are Handled)

When presenting a demo to any Bangalore business, the system dynamically populates the following parameters from the scraped Google Maps data:

| Parameter | Source from Lead Data | Dynamic Presentation Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Title** | `lead.name` | Displayed as the primary brand across hero and footer |
| **Locality Anchor** | `lead.locality` | Displayed as *"Serving [Locality], Bengaluru"* |
| **Social Proof Badge** | `lead.rating` + `lead.reviewsCount` | Displayed as *"[Rating]★ Verified Rating on Google ([Reviews] Reviews)"* |
| **Direct Contact Action** | `lead.phone` | Auto-wires all "Book", "Call", and "WhatsApp" buttons to their own phone number |
| **Industry Category** | `lead.nicheId` | Selects the corresponding Archetype template |

## 4. The Outreach & Closing Workflow

1. **The Teaser Pitch:** Send the lead a WhatsApp message referencing their strong Google reputation and offering a 1-minute live demo link.
2. **The Dynamic Showcase:** When they click the link, they see their own brand name, area, and customer rating showcased on a functional mobile demo.
3. **The Loss Aversion Hook:** They immediately recognize that having this live under their own domain (`.in` / `.com`) will capture direct customers instead of losing them to competitors.
4. **Handoff & Monetization:** Once approved, buy the domain, connect it to the template, collect payment (₹15,000–₹40,000), and transfer ownership.
