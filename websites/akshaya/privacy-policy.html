Privacy policy

How we handle your data

Effective 16 August 2026 · Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

What we collect

The data we ask for

When you use the booking form on this site, we collect your name, phone number, treatment of interest, and preferred day. We never ask for medical history through this website — that's discussed only in person, at the clinic, as part of your clinical examination. Basic, anonymised visit analytics (pages viewed, general location) are also collected to understand how the site is used.

Why we collect it

What it's used for

Your name, phone number, treatment interest, and preferred day are used for exactly one purpose: to call or WhatsApp you back and confirm an appointment. We don't sell this information, and we don't use it for anything beyond arranging your visit.

How long we keep it

Retention

Booking-form submissions are kept only as long as needed to arrange and follow up on your appointment, then deleted. Clinical records created once you're a patient are retained separately, under standard dental-practice recordkeeping requirements.

Your rights

What you can ask us to do

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data

Write to us at or call . We'll respond to any data request within a reasonable time.

Get in touch

Ready when you are

Call, WhatsApp, or book online — a member of the team will confirm your slot.
