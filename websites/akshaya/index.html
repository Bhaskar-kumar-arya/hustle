How we work

How laser fits into your implant and gum treatment

Light, not a blade

Soft-tissue procedures such as gum contouring use a focused laser beam in place of a scalpel.

Seals as it works

The laser seals small blood vessels along its path, which is why sutures are often not required.

Anaesthesia, often none

Many laser procedures are performed without local anaesthesia. Suitability is confirmed at examination.

Single-sitting disinfection

Laser-assisted canal disinfection is used in laser root canal treatment, in place of a multi-visit protocol.
